### Not-food check

Selected on validation data: **Maximum logit**, threshold 0.563 (keeps 95% of validation food photos). Rule: highest validation AUROC; ties: lower validation FPR at the target TPR, then the simpler score.

| Score | Validation AUROC | Validation FPR at 95% TPR | Test AUROC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum softmax probability | 0.912 | 46.0% | 0.913 |
| Maximum logit | 0.915 | 50.0% | 0.926 |
| Negative energy (log-sum-exp) | 0.901 | 59.0% | 0.915 |

On the test photos (used once): 56.0% of 200 non-food photos rejected, 94.7% of 150 food photos accepted; AUROC 0.926 (95% bootstrap CI 0.894 to 0.952).

| Collection | Test photos | Rejected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flowers-102 (flowers) | 100 | 37.0% |
| Oxford-IIIT Pet (cats and dogs) | 100 | 75.0% |

Score and threshold chosen on validation images (food) and a validation sample of non-food photos; the test images of both kinds were used once. Only obvious non-food (flowers, pets) was tested, so other foods are not covered by these numbers.

## C. Analysis of the selected model

Checkpoint check: the saved model, reloaded exactly as the app loads it, reproduced the test accuracy (96.0% against 96.0% reported).
